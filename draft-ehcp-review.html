SEND Support Lancashire

Draft EHCP Review Support

A draft EHCP can be difficult to follow, especially when it draws on several reports. We can help you review the document carefully, compare it with the information you have and prepare a clear list of questions or points you want to raise.

Practical help, clearly scoped

Support with organising information, preparing documents and planning questions. We are not a regulated law firm and do not provide reserved legal services, regulated legal advice or legal representation.

A structured read-through

We can help organise your comments across the plan: the child and family views, special educational needs, outcomes, provision and the named placement. We can also help you note where a description seems unclear, where information may be missing, or where you want to ask how a recommendation has been reflected.

Use the reports and your own knowledge

Gather recent professional reports, school information and examples from everyday life. A short example can help explain what a need looks like in practice, what support is already tried and what happens when it is not available. Our Section F support page focuses on recording provision-related questions.

What you receive

Depending on the agreed scope, support may include an organised set of comments, questions to discuss with the local authority, or help preparing your response. You remain responsible for checking the final document and deciding what to send. See our EHCP support and annual review pages for related help.
